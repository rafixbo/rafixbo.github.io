Course LMS Platform

In production

A course LMS combining SSC / HSC lectures, exams and notes with a general paid course-selling platform — video courses, payments and certificates, with a solid professional UI.

Year2026
StatusIn production
StackNext.js, TypeScript, Flutter

The problem

Lecture videos, exams and notes live in different places, and selling a paid course means stitching together a player, a payment link and a certificate by hand.

What was built

  • SSC / HSC track — lectures, exams and notes in one place, organised by subject and chapter.
  • Paid courses — video courses with enrolment, payments and certificates.
  • Professional UI — solid and clean, deliberately not kids-like; the first prototype was rejected on those grounds and rebuilt.
  • Stepping stone to Shikho Premium — the lessons from this LMS became the full production platform: Flutter app, NestJS backend and Bunny.net video.

The hard part

Balancing two audiences in one product — school students who need free lectures and exam practice, and paying learners who expect a polished course experience — without making either side feel like an afterthought.

Outcome

A working LMS that became the blueprint for Shikho Premium, the production learning platform.
